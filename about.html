About 786ServiceX

A vision to make healthcare easier to access and bring people closer to care.

OUR STORY

A Simple Idea. A Meaningful Purpose.

786ServiceX started with a simple idea: healthcare should be easier to access, and people should not feel forgotten when they need care.

I am working to turn this idea into a platform that connects people with healthcare professionals, supports elderly individuals and promotes health awareness.
